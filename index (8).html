Who We Are

ART Style — An Online Creative Agency

ART Style is an independent online creative agency built to bring creative talent and client projects together. We provide digital services through a coordinated network of editors, freelancers and developers. From video editing and visual design to website and app creation, every project is planned around the client's goals, scope and delivery requirements.

Our agency is open to skilled editors, creative freelancers and developers who want to collaborate on suitable projects. Client communication, project planning, creative execution and team coordination are handled through dedicated responsibilities so each assignment reaches the right specialist.

Online Creative ServicesVideo & Visual ProductionWebsite & App DevelopmentFreelancer Collaboration

ART Style was started and is jointly led by Subrat and Amit. Subrat coordinates client communication, marketing and external operations, while Amit oversees internal project allocation and editor/developer coordination. Both founders share equal authority and make key agency decisions together.
